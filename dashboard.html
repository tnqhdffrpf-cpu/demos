⚡ Big Story of the Week — Strait of Hormuz Standoff

Iran has offered to reopen the Strait of Hormuz — closed since the late-Feb US/Israel air war on Iran — in exchange for the US lifting its naval blockade and ending the war, with nuclear talks postponed for later. Sec. Rubio called the offer unacceptable on Sunday; Trump's national security team reviewed it Monday. Roughly 20% of global oil and LNG normally moves through this chokepoint, and Pentagon officials told Congress last week that fully clearing Iranian mines could take six months even after a deal.

Why it matters: this story hits markets (oil, shipping, defense), inflation, and US foreign-policy posture all at once. With shipping at ~5% of pre-war levels and a six-month mine-clearance tail, this isn't fading — even a "deal day" would be the start of the recovery story, not the end of the news cycle.

Prediction: grows. Watch for a Rubio counter-offer, an oil-price spike on any rejection headline, and renewed pressure on US homebuilders/refiners. Likely to dominate the week.
