Big Story of the Week

Strait of Hormuz crisis — every major outlet, all week

The Iran/Strait of Hormuz situation has hardened from a flare-up into the dominant story of 2026. Iran declared the Strait "closed" on March 4; since May 6, open transits have fallen to near zero. Brent crude is around $114/barrel, war-risk insurance premiums are 4–5× pre-crisis levels, and the IEA has labeled it the largest supply disruption in oil-market history. The U.S.–Israel Operation Epic Fury (Feb 28) killed Supreme Leader Khamenei, and the conflict has since absorbed Lebanon, the UAE, and shipping lanes off Oman.

Why it matters & what's next: This story grows, not fades. Two reasons: (1) every additional week with the Strait functionally closed compounds into Q2 inflation prints — the Dallas Fed has already modeled it as a multi-quarter shock — and (2) there's no clean off-ramp now that the Iranian leadership succession is unresolved. Expect this to dominate the news cycle through summer, with oil, defense, and shipping equities continuing to trade as the macro tell.
